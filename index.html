01 ABOUT

THE ROOTS

NOT FROM
SCRATCH.

01

ASTO.RUN continues the story of ASTORUN. The form changes. The energy stays.

The story began in Bila Tserkva with youth culture, sport, movement and a desire to build a space outside ordinary limits.

ASTORUN brought together parkour, freerun, workout, tricking, hip-hop, BMX, skateboarding and longboarding. But it was always bigger than sport — people, teams, events and a culture built from the ground up.

The organization was officially registered on March 6, 2013 as the Ukrainian Youth Association “ASTORUN”. Its activity goes back to the earlier period around 2009.

04 IDEA BASE

NEW IDEAS DRIVE THE PROCESS

IDEAS ARE
THE ENGINE.

New ideas are the engine behind everything we build.

ASTO.RUN creates individual entertainment concepts and turns them into playable formats for parties, performances, holidays, festivals, games and special events.

Sometimes the idea starts with a place. Sometimes with an audience, a challenge, a celebration or one simple question: what can people actually do here?

IDEA→FORMAT→EXPERIENCE→EVENT
THE PRINCIPLE DON'T JUST PUT ENTERTAINMENT
INTO AN EVENT. CREATE THE GAME.

We develop original mechanics that can be built around the audience, location, atmosphere and purpose of a particular event. One idea can become a small activation or a full-scale public format.

IDEA EXAMPLE / 01

ORIGINAL EVENT GAME

WET T-SHIRT
CHALLENGE

A simple beach game built around water, movement and competition.

01

FILL — each participant starts with a bucket of water.

02

SPLASH — participants pour water over the other players.

03

WRING — squeeze as much water as possible back into your own bucket.

04

WIN — the player who returns the most water wins the round.
